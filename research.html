Research projects

I work in CSCW and AI-mediated communication, studying how students, professional artists, and interns integrate generative AI into communication and work they already know, and what they protect, delegate, or disclose as they do. The studies are qualitative and phenomenon-first, built on interviews, critical incident technique, and participatory methods.
