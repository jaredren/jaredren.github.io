Research projects

My research sits in CSCW and AI-mediated communication. I study how people integrate generative AI into communication and work they already know how to do, and what they protect, delegate, or disclose as they do: students deciding when to let AI help write a message to a professor, professional artists setting limits on where a generative agent may enter their process, and interns resolving uncertainty about AI norms and disclosure at work. The studies are qualitative and phenomenon-first, built on interviews, critical incident technique, and participatory methods. Publication keys (p., n., m.) match the CV.
