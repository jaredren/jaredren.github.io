I am a second-year M.S. student in Human Centered Design & Engineering (HCI) at the University of Washington. I hold an Honours B.A. in Sociology and East Asian Studies from the University of Toronto.

My research asks how people integrate novel computational tools into professional practice, and what they protect, delegate, or disclose as they do. I approach this phenomenon-first, studying AI-mediated communication and human-AI interaction in the settings where people actually work and learn, mainly through interviews, critical incident technique, and participatory, community-based methods.

Research areas: Computer-Supported Cooperative Work (CSCW), Computer-Mediated Communication (CMC), AI-Mediated Communication, Human-AI Interaction, Future of Work.
